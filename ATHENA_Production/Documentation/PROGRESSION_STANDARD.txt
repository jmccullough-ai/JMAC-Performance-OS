Required ATHENA progression families:
- Sprinting
- Sled Push / Sprint
- Plyometrics
- Band Assisted / Resisted Jumps
- Depth Drop / Shock
- Olympic / Explosive

High/Low system:
Elastic, explosive, Olympic, sprint, sled, jump, throw, and plyometric work belongs first on high CNS days.
Armor, mobility, recovery, tissue capacity, and low output movement belongs later or on low CNS days.
